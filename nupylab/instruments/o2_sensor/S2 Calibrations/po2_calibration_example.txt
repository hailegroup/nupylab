# and keep it in this folder. The S2 GUI loads the newest file named that way
# when it starts, and the pO2 panel can load any other.
#
# pO2 = 0.2095 * 10 ** (20158 * ((E - b) / T - a))
#
#   E : pO2 sensor potential in volts, as measured by the Keithley 182
#   T : pO2 sensor temperature in kelvin, as measured by the Keithley 2182
#
# The coefficients are those of the sensor potential against its temperature,
# E = a T + b, in the lettering S2's LabVIEW program uses:
#
#   a : slope in volts per kelvin
#   b : intercept in volts
#
# Watch the units when entering them: swapping the two gives a pO2 that is
# wrong by orders of magnitude rather than obviously broken. `slope` and
# `intercept` may be written in place of `a` and `b`.
#
# Blank lines are ignored, as is anything after a # or ! comment character.
# A name and its value may be separated by =, :, , or whitespace.
#
